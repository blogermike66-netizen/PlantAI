About PlantAI

PlantAI helps gardeners, farmers and plant lovers find out what a plant is from a single photo. Take a picture of a leaf or upload one, and our AI suggests the plant's name along with simple care advice.

How it works

1. Add a photoUpload a picture or take one with your camera.
2. IdentifyPress "Identify Plant" and the photo is analysed by AI.
3. Read the resultSee the answer and find it again later in Your Scans.

Who runs PlantAI

PlantAI is built and run by Mike Gambatuka, an independent web developer, as an easy tool for anyone who wants to understand their plants better.

Please note

Results are generated by AI and can be wrong, especially with blurry or dark photos. Treat them as a helpful guide and check with a local plant expert before treating or removing a plant. See our Privacy Policy to learn how photos are handled.
