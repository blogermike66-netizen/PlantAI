Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

PlantAI ("we", "us") is a website that helps you identify plants from a photo. This policy explains what information is used when you use PlantAI, and what choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

1. Information we handle

  • Photos you upload or take. When you press "Identify Plant", the photo is sent to our server so it can be analysed.
  • Camera access. If you use "Take a Photo", your browser asks permission to use your camera. The camera is only used to take the picture you choose, and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Your scan history. The result of each scan and a small copy of the photo are saved in your own browser (local storage) so you can see them on the "Your Scans" page. This data stays on your device.
  • Technical data. Like most websites, our server and hosting provider may record technical information such as IP address, browser type and the time of a request, for security and to keep the site working.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or payment details to use the scanner.

2. How we use photos

Photos are used only to identify the plant and show you the result. Our server passes the photo to an AI service to produce the answer and sends the answer back to you. Our server does not save your photos.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Gemini. Photos you submit are sent to Google's Gemini API to analyse the image. Google's handling of this data is described in the Gemini API terms and Google's Privacy Policy, and content sent through a free-tier service may be used by Google to improve its products.
  • Google Fonts. Our pages load fonts from Google, which may receive your IP address when the page loads.
  • Hosting provider. Our hosting provider processes requests to deliver the website.

4. Cookies, local storage and advertising

PlantAI uses your browser's local storage to keep your scan history. This is not used to track you.

If advertising is shown on this site, third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or learn more at aboutads.info.

5. Your choices

  • You can delete your scan history at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • You can block camera access in your browser. The upload option still works.
  • You can contact us to ask a question about your data or to request that we delete anything we hold about you.

6. Children

PlantAI is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will remove it.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information handled by the site, including sending data over a secure connection where the site is hosted with HTTPS. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Not professional advice

Plant and disease results are generated by AI and may be wrong. They are for general information only and are not a replacement for advice from an agricultural or horticultural professional.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
